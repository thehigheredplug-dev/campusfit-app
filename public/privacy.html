CampusFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

CampusFit helps students explore colleges, assess college fit and affordability, and discover scholarship opportunities. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use CampusFit.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CampusFit, information may include:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and account identifiers used to sign in.
  • Student information: information you choose to provide for college matching or affordability-related features.
  • Search information: college and scholarship searches or preferences you submit.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, diagnostic, and usage information needed to operate and maintain the service.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information unless it is specifically required for a feature.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Authenticate your sign-in and help protect account security.
  • Provide college matching, search, and scholarship discovery features.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve CampusFit.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sign-In Services and Third Parties

CampusFit uses Firebase Authentication and may support Google sign-in. These providers process information as needed to authenticate your account and deliver their services, subject to their own privacy terms. CampusFit may also rely on hosting, database, and infrastructure providers to operate the service.

CampusFit does not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed where necessary to operate the service, protect users, or comply with the law.

4. Scholarships and External Websites

Scholarship and college information may include links to third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices and terms. Review them before sharing information or submitting an application.

5. Data Storage and Security

Information is handled using service providers and technical safeguards intended to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide CampusFit, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. To ask a question about your personal information or request account deletion, contact us using the email below. Some information may need to be retained where legally required.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. Children's Privacy

CampusFit is intended for students and other users who can lawfully use the service. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as CampusFit changes. When updates are made, the date at the top of this page will be revised.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: unclej@thehigheredplug.com

This page describes general privacy practices and is not legal advice. The policy should be reviewed against CampusFit's actual data handling and applicable legal requirements before being treated as final.
